Tex. Est. Code § 309.051: within 90 days after the personal representative qualifies. Qualification date 2026-09-01 is on the docket, so the return date is fixed whether or not anyone is counting.

What has to be filed, in order

  1. Inventory, Appraisement and List of Claims, sworn, filed with the clerk of Probate Court No. 1 on or before 2026-11-30. § 309.051, § 309.052.
  2. The appraisement. Real property at market value as of the date of death, not the appraisal roll. Attach the HCAD account numbers so the appraisers can find each parcel.
  3. Affidavit in lieu of inventory, if the will waives it and no creditor or devisee demands one. § 309.056. Cheaper and faster, but only if the will allows it.
  4. Notice to creditors, if not already run, and the publisher's affidavit filed. § 308.051. A claim that lands before the inventory is a debt line on the same form.
