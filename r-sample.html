What the page tells you

Every row that matches the name, the county each one sits in, the exact amount, and the claim route for that county in order. Marshall County is the OCC escrow claim form first, then the Treasurer's unclaimed property division if the money has already moved, with an affidavit of heirship if the owner is dead.

What it does not do

It does not prove the chain to the recorded deed. Texas, New Mexico and North Dakota registers stay behind a bot wall and are not in a $99 answer. And it does not file: the claim pack, the filing and the correspondence are the 20 percent, and the $99 comes off that 20 percent.

If the search comes back empty

You still get a page. It names the operator suspense route and the two offices to write instead. The money is often in the operator's suspense account rather than the state's, and that route has no cap on the fee at all.
